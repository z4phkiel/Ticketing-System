# Knowledge base

The AI may answer tickets only from these articles. Edit this file to change what it knows.

## Account

**Reset a forgotten password.** On the sign-in page click "Forgot password?", enter your account email,
and follow the link we send. The link expires after 1 hour. Check your spam folder if it does not arrive
within 5 minutes.

**Change your display name.** Go to Settings > Profile, edit your name, and click Save.

**Change your email address.** Go to Settings > Profile > Email, enter the new address, and confirm it
from the verification email we send to the new address.

**Delete your account.** Go to Settings > Account > Delete account. Deletion is permanent after 14 days;
signing in during those 14 days cancels it.

## Billing

**Update your payment method.** Go to Settings > Billing > Payment method and click "Replace card".
The new card is used from the next invoice.

**Download invoices.** Go to Settings > Billing > Invoices. Every invoice can be downloaded as a PDF.

**When am I charged?** Plans renew on the same day each month (or year for annual plans). You get a
reminder email 3 days before an annual renewal.

**Cancel a subscription.** Go to Settings > Billing > Plan > Cancel plan. You keep access until the end
of the current billing period. Refund requests must be handled by a support agent.

## Technical

**The app is slow or a page will not load.** Refresh the page, then try a private window. If that works,
clear your browser cache for this site. Supported browsers: the latest two versions of Chrome, Edge,
Firefox, and Safari.

**Install the app on your phone or computer.** In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon in the address
bar or the "Install app" button. On iPhone, open the site in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".

**Notifications are not arriving.** Check Settings > Notifications in the app and make sure your browser
allows notifications for this site.

**Export your data.** Go to Settings > Data > Export. You get a ZIP file by email within 24 hours.

## Always handled by an agent

Refunds, hacked or compromised accounts, suspicious logins, two-factor authentication lockouts, legal
requests, outages, and data loss.
